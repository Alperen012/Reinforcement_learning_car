    Reward weights

    These change the reward you see, not how trained agents drive (they learned from the defaults). To change behaviour, retrain, e.g. racing-gym train -c configs/ppo_classic.toml -s env.reward.crash=0.

    How it works
    Agent & policy
    The driver is a small neural network (17 inputs → 64 → 64 → 2 outputs). It maps what it sees to what it does. Nobody wrote driving rules into it.
    Observation
    9 lidar rays (distance to the track edge), speed, wheel angle, how far it points away from the track direction, how far it is from the centre line, and how much the track bends ahead.
    Action
    Two numbers in [-1, 1]: steering and throttle (negative throttle brakes, then reverses).
    Reward
    +1 per 10 px of progress along the track, −0.05 per step, −20 for leaving the track, +10 per lap. The agent only ever sees these numbers. Learning means finding behaviour that makes their sum large.
    Training
    Drag the training stage slider. Untrained, the network barely moves. Early on it drives straight into walls. A few hundred crashes later it races. That is reinforcement learning.
